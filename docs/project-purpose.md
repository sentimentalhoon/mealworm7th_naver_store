# Project Purpose

## Summary

밀웜7번가 needs a public website that can be discovered by Google and Naver, explain the brand and products clearly, and guide visitors to the Naver Smartstore for purchase.

The site is not meant to replace Smartstore checkout. It is the official information and routing hub for search users who cannot reliably land on the Smartstore directly from Google or Naver.

## Audience

- Buyers looking for live mealworms or superworms.
- Buyers looking for dried mealworms or dried superworms.
- Repeat customers who need a quick path to Smartstore purchase.
- Search engines that need structured, crawlable information about the brand and products.

## Products

- Live mealworms.
- Live superworms.
- Dried mealworms.
- Dried superworms.

Product information must stay aligned with the current Smartstore listings and detail pages.

## Success Criteria

- Visitors can immediately identify the site as the official 밀웜7번가 website.
- Visitors understand the product categories before leaving for Smartstore.
- Important shipping, storage, exchange, and refund conditions are visible before purchase.
- Google and Naver can crawl indexable pages without JavaScript-only redirects.
- Search pages contain original useful content, not copied Smartstore fragments alone.
- The Naver Smartstore purchase path is clear and honest.

## Non-Goals

- Do not build an independent checkout unless explicitly requested.
- Do not create keyword-only landing pages.
- Do not duplicate the same page many times for small keyword variations.
- Do not hide the Smartstore relationship.
- Do not make medical, veterinary, or guaranteed-effect claims.

## Maintenance Notes

When Smartstore policy, shipping method, cutoff time, compensation policy, product quantity, or packaging changes, update:

- Product HTML/detail assets.
- Public site content.
- `docs/search-smartstore-strategy.md` if routing or SEO implications change.
- `README.txt` if build/export workflow changes.
